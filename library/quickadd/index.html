    Barcodes on new items
    Digits per barcode:  Next barcode:
    Same-length numbers are what Koha's label printer needs for a range, so numbers are zero-padded (0000042). Seven or more digits keeps scanners from misreading a short all-number barcode as the pharmacy "Code 32" format.
